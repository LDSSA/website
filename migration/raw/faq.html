FAQ

1 - What do you mean by strong quantitative skills?

You should be comfortable around numbers, quickly understanding a real life problem and translate it into something measurable & workable. Usually is the kind of skills that results from having an education or working experience in the fields of (but not only) Engineering, Physics, Mathematics, Management, Economics, etc.. 

 

2 - Will I have any kind of teaching support, if I need, during the months following the Bootcamp Days?

Definitely yes! This is one of the  strongest things we want to build during the Academy experience: the community effect! We are working hard to make sure you have access to a community of colleagues, teachers and staff that will be there to help you in whatever way it's possible. Even you will be able to help others! 

 

3 - Is there any financial support if I can't afford the student fees?

We are in the process of deciding this. Please  check this page soon.

 

4 - What will be the size of the students group?

We are expecting a maximum of 50 students. The idea is to have a high teacher-student ratio, and to ensure everyone gets enough support. 

 

5 - Are meals included in the fee?

Yes, lunch and snacks during the day. 

 

6 - Will there be any kind of internship possibility at the end of the school?

We are working hard to surprise you on this topic. More news will come soon. 

 

7 - What language will the classes be held in?

English. 

 

8 - Will you teach python? 

Yes but enrolments for the Data Science Prep Course for Batch 3 are now closed. 
Want to enrol next time? Subscribe here to our newsletter to stay updated on all new dates! 

 

9 - I don’t know any data science, will I be able to do the classes? 

Yes! The classes are directed at people who are giving their first steps in the data science world. 

 

10 - I can come to the Bootcamp days, but can’t make it to the hackathons. Should I apply anyway? 

﻿Presence in the bootcamp and Hackathon #1 is compulsory, as is the capstone.
Of hackathons 2 through 5, you are allowed to do one remote if the need is justified, and (if you really, really have to) miss one.



11 - Will laptops be provided? 

No, you must bring your laptop with you

 

12  - When will we install the necessary software? 

Setup must be done before arrival, we will provide step by step instructions, and remote support. 


13 - Do you have a refund policy?

Sorry, since the payment and course start dates are so close, we're not doing refunds.


14 - How do I know if my python skills are good enough?

A good option would be doing our test. In case you want to know, the main topics we cover on this test are the following: 

- if and else statements
- for loops
- handle arrays and tuples (including slicing)
- handle lists and dictionaries (including list comprehension)
- string manipulation
- data types (like int, float, string and how to convert variables between them)
- define and use functions
- lambda functions and map
- try except statements
- basics of OOP (like what's a class, how to instantiate one and use methods from the class)
