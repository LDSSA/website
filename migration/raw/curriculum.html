ORGANISATIONS THAT MAKE

OUR ACADEMIES POSSIBLE

BOOTCAMPS

The Bootcamp is an intensive, 2 day event, where students and teachers will be in the same building for 10 hours per day, and the students will go through their first 20 Learning Units. 


HACKATHONS

Hackathons are longer and less structured exercises, often done in teams, in which the students must complete a relatively advanced project. Hackathons require the students to make use of the skills they learned in the Learning Units, but also to excel at teamwork and communication.
