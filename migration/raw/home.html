Data Scientists are one of the most required and highly paid professionals today. They swim in the heaps of data our society generates and make discoveries that drive business decisions. It’s been called the coolest profession of the 21st century.


WHAT IS THE LISBON DATA SCIENCE ACADEMY ABOUT?

The LDSA is a non-profit organization dedicated to Data Science, 100% run by volunteers. It is responsible for the Data Science Starters Academy which focuses on introductory material for those who want to become entry-level data scientists.
