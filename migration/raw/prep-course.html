1. What is the LDSA Prep Course about?

The Prep Course was designed to teach you everything you need to know before joining the Data Science Starters Academy. It was created to be taken 100% remotely. This means that it's fully taught online, with new content every week, and instructors available to help you. 


2. What topics are covered?

We start by teaching you how to program in Python. We'll then go through some Linear Algebra basics and we'll show you how to translate them into coding using Numpy. We'll also cover some software development utilities like how to use the command line, using a text editor for programming,  version control with git and Python modules, and packaging. We'll wrap it up with a final project where you'll be putting everything you've learned into practice.


3. When does the course start?

The more news on this soon. Subscribe to our newsletter on our main page to receive the news firsthand.


4. How much does it cost?

The Prep Course is free for everyone!


5. What is the recommended time commitment?

If you have no prior knowledge of any of the subjects, you should be prepared to spend an absolute minimum of 10 hours per week on the course. We understand this might sound like a lot. However, this course was not designed to give you some brief ideas on Python programming. Instead, we expect that you will fully grasp all fundamental concepts and practice enough to instinctively solve programming problems. If you already have a certain degree of knowledge of some topics, we expect the time required will be less. Finally, keep in mind that this recommendation does not include the preparation time for the admission test for the Starters Academy.


6. Is there any software/app I need to be able to do the course?

Yes, there is some software you’ll need to install, but we will provide you with detailed guidelines on how to do it. This requires you to have a computer/laptop with admin rights.


7. How does the application process work?

Once you sign up, we will share with you the application form. You will need to answer all of the questions, which will be evaluated to decide whether you will be accepted. Keep in mind that we will not test any technical or prior knowledge. This is for us to assess your motivation and whether you will be to undertake an accelerated learning experience. Dates about applications will be disclosed soon. 


8. By doing the course, do I have a 100% chance to get into the Lisbon Data Science Starters Academy?

No, doing or completing the course does not guarantee you a slot in the Academy. What we are confident to say is that doing the course will increase your chance to get in. Last year, out of 50 people who got accepted into the Academy, 25 people went through the prep course, and many of them had never been exposed to programming before.


9. I’m an absolute n00b. Will I be able to do the Prep Course?

This course is designed for people without any prior knowledge or experience in the field. This is our diversity effort to enable everyone to enter the amazing world of Data Science.


10. Will I get a certificate at the end?

Not at this time. However, we are sure that, more than the certificate you could get, your biggest takeaway from this course will be the knowledge you've acquired and how it will help you navigate the world of Data Science.


We kindly remind you that - although most of our students are able to successfully find a job in Data Science - we can't guarantee it will actually land you a job and a career in tech. Additionally, the Starters Academy was designed to be taken as a sidekick, not your main activity.
