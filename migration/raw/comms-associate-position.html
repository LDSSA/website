Lisbon Data Science Academy is searching  for a Communications Associate (voluntary position)

The role:

We need help spreading the word about the Academy and managing our social presence, including some content creation. For this we need someone passionate about bringing affordable tech education to students and professionals, someone with fresh ideas and a willingness to learn, but teach too.

Who we are:

Lisbon Data Science Academy is a non-profit, volunteer-based organisation bringing affordable education to students and tech professionals. We are proud to say that amongst our volunteers we have a few of the best data scientist in Portugal, successful entrepreneurs, engineers, founders, CEOs and CTOs.

Last year we ran the first data science course in Portugal. It was a great success and an amazing experience for the whole team, where we got to work with industry leaders such as Microsoft, Farfetch, Uniplaces. 

This year we’re set on providing an even better experience for our students and expanding our network of partners even further. That’s where you come in!

What’s in it for you:

In return we offer over 200 years of our cumulative experience in the tech industry. 

  • You will be part of all our weekly meetings and will be able to see first hand the inner workings of an educational startup. 

  • You will have a voice in building the Lisbon Data Science Academy brand 

  • You’ll become a part of our Mastermind and make friends for life

  • You’ll increase your network with people from startups and tech companies.

  • We will add you as a collaborator in our LinkedIn organisation and write a recommendation that might just be the tipping point towards hiring for your next clients.


Time investment:

  • This positions will be open from now until September 2018. 

  • It will require around 5 hours per week in the first 2 months and between 2 and 4 hours per week after that.

  • Once a month, you’ll need to attend our hackathons for  few hours (always a Sunday). Locations and dates are to be decided, but previous locations included the Lisbon offices of Microsoft, Farfetch, Uniplaces and Outsystems, to name a few.
